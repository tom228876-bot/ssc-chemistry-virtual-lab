# Apparatus GLB assets

## Original local models
The original nine lightweight educational apparatus models in this directory are authored for SSC Chemistry Virtual Lab and dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0 Universal:
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

These are: test tube, microscope, tripod stand, thermometer, spring balance, vernier calipers, micrometer screw gauge, convex lens and concave mirror.

## Imported CC0 models
The following nine models are from MilkAndBanana's *Lab Assets* pack (created 2022-01-31), distributed under Creative Commons Zero (CC0):
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Source repository:
https://github.com/aamodpaudel/ARISE-AI-Powered-Virtual-Laboratory-for-Accessibility-Microsoft-ICJ-Winner-

Source license text is preserved in `CC0-SOURCE-LICENSE.txt`.

Imported models: beaker, conical flask, test tube, pipette, thermometer, tripod stand, Bunsen burner, funnel/buret and dropper. They are served locally from this repository; the manifest does not depend on third-party asset-host URLs.

All models have no external texture dependencies. They are educational visual assets and are not dimensionally certified laboratory equipment. Apparatus without an exact matching CC0 model continues to use the existing procedural fallback rather than an unrelated model.
